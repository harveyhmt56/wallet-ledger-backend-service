## What held on the running image

- 100 concurrent debits of 10 against 500 → exactly 50/50, contiguous winner sequences, ledger sum 0; 100 copies of one key → one posting; 40 opposing transfers conserved the total; competing refunds, provisions, daily/promotion/reward claims → one winner each with the documented loser code; 80 mixed operations matched an in-memory oracle.
- Seeded 3-day streak → 20 concurrent claims on day four → one `200` (`streak 4`, amount 40), 19 × `DAILY_ALREADY_CLAIMED`. Capacity-one promotion paid once, second player `409 PROMOTION_EXHAUSTED`. Disabled reward/promotion refused with no claim row or money; paid once after re-enabling. Suspended player refused on credit, debit, incoming transfer, completion and refund; accepted again after reactivation. Wallet at `Long.MAX_VALUE`: incoming transfer and direct credit → `409 BALANCE_LIMIT` with both balances untouched.
- Chaos: Redis frozen 32 s → requests served in 0.28 s, `wallet.rate_limit.degraded` 0→4. Kafka stopped → credit succeeded, relay drained after restart, nothing lost. Kafka poisoned with 2 malformed + 13 hostile numeric events (including `schemaVersion 4294967297`, which the pre-F-03 coercion accepted as 1) → all 15 quarantined before any write, projection unchanged, later genuine events applied on every partition. PostgreSQL stopped → write `503 DEPENDENCY_UNAVAILABLE`, `Retry-After: 1`, same key succeeded exactly once after restart. PostgreSQL frozen 32 s → new requests fail fast in 5.1 s, the held request completed once after 37.4 s. Process `SIGKILL`ed with 298 requests in flight → 0 orphaned reservations, 300/300 keys resolved to one journal each, receipts replayed byte-identical.
- After every destructive step `audit_ledger_integrity()` returned no rows and `/v1/admin/reconciliation` answered `consistent:true`. Corruption is detected: a superuser drift 40→41 with the trigger disabled produced `consistent:false` and a `wallet_tail` audit row, and the commit trigger refused a posting against that wallet.
- Quota 5/min: sixth request `429 RATE_LIMITED`, `Retry-After: 60`, no key reserved, nothing posted; same key succeeded once after the window. Authorization matrix 14 endpoints × 5 callers: every denial 401/403 problem+json. README demo reproduced byte-identical.
